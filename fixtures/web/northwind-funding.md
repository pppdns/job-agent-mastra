# Northwind Robotics raises €40M Series B

Published 10 March 2026 · TechWire

Lisbon-based Northwind Robotics has raised a €40 million Series B led by Example Ventures, the company announced on 4 March 2026. The startup says it will use the money to expand into Germany and grow its engineering team.

The company did not disclose revenue. A spokesperson said it is "not yet profitable".
