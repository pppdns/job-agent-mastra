# Senior Backend Engineer (TypeScript)

Lindwurm Health · Remote (EU)

## About us

Lindwurm Health builds appointment and prescription software for independent pharmacies in Austria, Germany and Slovenia. We are 75 people and fully remote across the EU.

## What you will build

* Customer-facing product features in our TypeScript (Node.js, NestJS) backend: prescription refills, pharmacy inventory sync and patient notifications.
* APIs for our React and mobile apps, together with designers and product managers.
* Integrations with national e-prescription systems.

## What we look for

* Five or more years of backend development in TypeScript or another typed language.
* Solid PostgreSQL and API design skills.
* Comfort working directly with product managers on feature scope.

## What we offer

* Fully remote within the EU; no office days and no relocation needed.
* Gross salary EUR 7,600 per month, paid 12 times a year, plus a EUR 3,000 yearly bonus.
* 27 days of holiday, a new laptop of your choice.
