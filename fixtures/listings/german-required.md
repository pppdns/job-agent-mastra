# Senior Backend Engineer, Plattform-Team

Hafenwerk Logistik GmbH · Remote (Deutschland)

## Über uns / About us

Hafenwerk Logistik digitalises freight handling for inland ports along the Rhine. We are a team of 150, headquartered in Duisburg, with engineers working remotely across Germany.

## Your role

* Develop and operate the backend services that schedule barge slots and crane capacity.
* Run our services on Kubernetes (on-prem OpenShift) together with the operations team.
* Modernise a large Java 17 / Spring Boot codebase; new services are written in Kotlin.
* Work closely with port operators, logistics planners and our customer-support team.

## Requirements

* Five or more years of backend development, ideally in Java or Kotlin.
* Experience with Kubernetes or OpenShift, PostgreSQL and message queues.
* **Fluent German (C1 or better) is required**: the team, all documentation and every customer meeting are in German.
* Willingness to travel to a port site about twice a year.

## What we offer

* Fully remote within Germany; no office days.
* Salary EUR 88,000 – 98,000 per year.
* Company pension scheme, JobRad bicycle leasing, 30 days of holiday.
