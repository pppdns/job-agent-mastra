# Staff Platform Engineer

Brennholz Energy · Remote (Germany)

## About Brennholz

Brennholz Energy optimises battery storage for solar parks. We are 90 people, with our engineering hub in Munich.

## What you will do

* Design the next generation of our Kubernetes-based edge platform that runs on 2,000 battery sites.
* Own fleet-wide rollout tooling written in Rust and Go.
* Set technical direction for the platform group (four engineers) as a senior individual contributor.
* Partner with the hardware team on secure device provisioning.

## Our team culture

We believe great platforms are built together. The platform group works from our Munich office Tuesday to Thursday, which is where design sessions, pairing and our weekly demo happen. Mondays and Fridays are flexible.

## What you bring

* Nine or more years in infrastructure or systems engineering.
* Rust or Go in production; Kubernetes at the edge or in constrained environments is a plus.
* English is our working language.

## What we offer

* Salary EUR 110,000 – 130,000 per year plus virtual shares.
* Relocation support to Munich.
* 30 days of holiday.
