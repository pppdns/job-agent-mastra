# Engineering Manager, Platform

Ostrava Mobility · Remote (EU)

## About us

Ostrava Mobility builds fleet-management software for electric bus operators in 14 European countries. We are 300 people; engineering is fully remote across the EU.

## The role

You will lead the Platform team: seven engineers who run our Kubernetes infrastructure, CI/CD and observability. This is a people-leadership role first.

* Hire, coach and grow the seven engineers on the team; run one-to-ones and performance reviews.
* Own the team's roadmap and balance it against requests from twelve product teams.
* Represent platform in architecture reviews and incident retrospectives.
* Stay technical: expect around 10–15% of your time on hands-on work such as design reviews or small tooling fixes, but not on the critical path.

## What you bring

* Eight or more years in software engineering, including at least two leading people, or a strong wish to move into management with relevant mentoring experience.
* Background in Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure and developer platforms.
* Calm incident leadership and clear written communication in English.

## What we offer

* Fully remote within the EU, with two team meetups a year.
* Salary EUR 105,000 – 125,000 per year.
* 28 days of holiday, EUR 2,500 training budget.
