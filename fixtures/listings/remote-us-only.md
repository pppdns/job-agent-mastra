# Senior Infrastructure Engineer — Remote

Tallgrass Data · Remote

## Who we are

Tallgrass Data provides data pipelines for agricultural co-ops. We are a 200-person company, remote since 2020, and our infrastructure team of eight keeps 300 customer pipelines running.

## What you'll do

* Operate and improve our Kubernetes clusters on GCP (GKE), with Terraform for everything.
* Build self-service tooling in Go so product teams can ship without tickets.
* Lead the migration from our legacy job scheduler to Argo Workflows.
* Take part in an on-call rotation, one week in eight.

## What you'll need

* Seven or more years in infrastructure or backend engineering.
* Deep Kubernetes and Terraform experience; Go or Python.
* Experience running stateful workloads (PostgreSQL, Kafka) on Kubernetes.

## Compensation and benefits

* Base salary USD 175,000 – 205,000, plus equity.
* Health, dental and vision coverage, 401(k) with matching.
* Home-office stipend of USD 1,500.

## Logistics

This is a fully remote position. Candidates must reside in the United States and be authorized to work there; we are unable to employ people outside the US, including as contractors, and we do not sponsor visas. Core hours are 10am – 3pm US Central Time.
