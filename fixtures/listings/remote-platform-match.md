# Senior Platform Engineer (Remote, EU)

Fully remote within the EU · No office days

## About Fernhollow Systems

Fernhollow Systems runs a scheduling platform for veterinary clinics across Europe. We are a team of 40, fully remote since day one, with colleagues in Germany, Poland, Spain and the Netherlands.

## What you will do

* Own our Kubernetes platform on two cloud regions: cluster upgrades, autoscaling and cost.
* Build the developer platform our four product teams deploy through: CI pipelines, preview environments, golden-path templates.
* Run the observability stack and set service level objectives with the product teams.
* Join a light on-call rotation (one week in eight, follow-the-sun with our US contractor).

## What we are looking for

* Six or more years of backend or infrastructure engineering.
* Deep Kubernetes and Terraform experience; Go or TypeScript for tooling.
* You like making other engineers faster more than shipping product features.

## What we offer

* Fully remote from anywhere in the EU; we meet in person twice a year.
* Salary EUR 85,000 – 100,000 per year plus equity.
* 30 days of paid leave.
