# Senior Infrastructure Engineer

Harrowgate Health · Remote (UK)

## About us

Harrowgate Health builds scheduling software for NHS clinics. We are 80 people, fully remote across the UK.

## The role

* Look after our Kubernetes clusters and Terraform-managed cloud accounts.
* Improve deployment safety and incident response for the product teams.
* Help us meet NHS data security requirements.

## You bring

* Five or more years in infrastructure or platform engineering.
* Kubernetes, Terraform and Linux in production.

## Working here

* Remote within the UK only: you must live in the United Kingdom and already have the right to work here. We cannot employ people based outside the UK.
* Salary GBP 85,000 – 100,000.
