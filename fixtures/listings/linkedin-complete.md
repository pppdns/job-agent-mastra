### Dana Example

#### Talent Partner at Northwind Robotics

Northwind Robotics builds warehouse robots that move and sort parcels for mid-sized logistics companies. Our fleet software runs on thousands of robots across Europe, and the platform team keeps that software deployable, observable and safe.

**What you will do**

* Own the internal developer platform used by six product teams: CI pipelines, preview environments and deployment tooling.
* Run and evolve our Kubernetes clusters across two cloud regions, including capacity planning and cost reviews.
* Build the observability stack (metrics, logs, traces) and define service level objectives with product teams.
* Join a shared on-call rotation for platform services, roughly one week in six.
* Mentor two mid-level engineers and review designs across teams.

**What we are looking for**

* Six or more years building and operating production infrastructure.
* Strong experience with Kubernetes, Terraform and one major cloud provider.
* Comfortable writing services and tooling in Go or TypeScript.
* Experience introducing SLOs and incident review practices.
* Clear written communication in English; Portuguese is not required.

**Nice to have**

* Experience with fleets of edge devices or robotics.
* Contributions to open source infrastructure projects.

**What we offer**

* Hybrid work: three days a week in our Lisbon office.
* Learning budget and conference travel.
* Private health insurance.

Northwind Robotics is an equal opportunity employer.
