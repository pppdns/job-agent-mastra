# Senior Platform Engineer

Remote (US)

## About Northwind Freight

Northwind Freight is a trucking marketplace connecting shippers with carriers across North America. Our platform team runs the systems that match loads, price routes and track deliveries in real time for thousands of carriers.

## Responsibilities

* Operate our AWS infrastructure and Kubernetes clusters.
* Build CI/CD tooling and self-service environments for product engineers.
* Improve reliability, observability and incident response across services.
* Partner with security on infrastructure hardening and compliance work.
* Participate in an on-call rotation.

## Requirements

* Five or more years in infrastructure or platform engineering roles.
* Deep experience with AWS, Terraform and Kubernetes in production.
* Strong programming skills in Python or Go.
* Experience running incident reviews and defining reliability targets.
* Excellent communication skills and comfort working across time zones.

## Benefits

* Fully remote within the United States, with quarterly team meetups.
* Health, dental and vision coverage plus a home office stipend.
* Generous parental leave and a yearly learning budget for courses and conferences.
