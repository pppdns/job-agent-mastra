# Staff Backend Engineer, Payments

Lumen Ledger · Remote

## About us

Lumen Ledger builds invoicing and payment reconciliation software for small accounting firms. We are a team of 60 spread across several countries.

## The role

* Design and run the services that match bank transactions to invoices.
* Lead the migration of our payment pipeline from a monolith to event-driven services.
* Work with product and support on reliability issues reported by customers.
* Define service level objectives for the reconciliation pipeline and lead incident reviews.
* Review designs from other teams and help set backend engineering standards across the company.
* Mentor two senior engineers and help us grow the payments team from five to eight people over the next year.

## You bring

* Eight or more years of backend engineering, ideally in fintech or payments.
* Production experience with PostgreSQL, Kafka or a similar event log, and Go, Java or Kotlin.
* Experience mentoring engineers and leading cross-team technical work.
* Familiarity with double-entry bookkeeping, PSD2 or bank file formats such as CAMT is a plus.
* Clear written communication: most of our collaboration is asynchronous and in English.

## Working here

* Remote-first: we collaborate in European time zones and meet in person twice a year.
* Competitive compensation and benefits.
* Four weeks of holiday.
