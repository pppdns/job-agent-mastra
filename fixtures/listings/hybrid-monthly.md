# Senior Platform Engineer (Go)

Quillstone · Berlin, Germany (Hybrid)

## About Quillstone

Quillstone builds contract-management software for mid-sized law firms in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. We are 120 people, about 45 of them in engineering, and profitable since 2024.

## Working model

We call it hybrid, but it is light: the whole engineering team meets in our Berlin Kreuzberg office one day a month for planning and a team lunch. The rest of the time you work from home or wherever you like within Germany. We pay for a co-working desk if you want one.

## What you will do

* Own the Kubernetes platform (EKS, two regions) and its upgrade path.
* Build internal tooling in Go: deployment pipelines, a service catalogue and cost dashboards.
* Run the observability stack (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry) and help teams set SLOs.
* Share an on-call rotation with five other platform engineers, one week in six.
* Write design documents and review infrastructure changes across teams.

## What we are looking for

* Six or more years in backend or infrastructure engineering.
* Production experience with Kubernetes and Terraform.
* Go for tooling and services; Rust is a bonus.
* English is our working language. German is helpful for the occasional customer call but not required.

## What we offer

* Salary EUR 92,000 – 108,000 per year, plus a yearly bonus of up to 10%.
* 30 days of paid leave, EUR 2,000 learning budget.
* Individual contributor career track up to principal engineer.
