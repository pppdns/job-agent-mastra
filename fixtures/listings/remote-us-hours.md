# Senior Platform Engineer

Quillmark · Remote

## About us

Quillmark makes document automation software for law firms. Our customers are mostly in North America, and the company is headquartered in Boston.

## The role

* Run and improve our Kubernetes platform on AWS, written in Terraform.
* Own CI/CD, observability and on-call tooling for twelve product engineers.
* Work with security on SOC 2 controls for the infrastructure.

## You bring

* Six or more years in platform or infrastructure engineering.
* Production Kubernetes, Terraform and one of Go or Python.

## Working here

* Fully remote. We hire in the United States, Canada and Europe (as employees or through an employer of record).
* Most of the team works US Eastern hours; we ask for at least four hours of overlap with 9am – 5pm Eastern.
* Base salary USD 150,000 – 175,000.
